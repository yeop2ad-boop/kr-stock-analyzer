Privacy Policy (English summary)

Slime King: Roguelite RPG (package kr.marketmap.slimetower) has no sign-up or login. To run the leaderboard, the app sends your chosen nickname, a random player ID it creates, and your game records (tier, floor, score, level, outfit color, equipped relic) to the developer's server; save data is uploaded only when you create a transfer code and is deleted after 30 days. Purchases are processed by Google Play; the developer's server receives the product ID, purchase token and order number to verify the purchase and keeps them for 400 days. Play Integrity results and tamper/root/emulator/debugger checks are sent to prevent cheating. Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects the advertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions and crash/diagnostic data. The server is hosted on Cloudflare; data may be processed outside Korea. Game progress otherwise stays on your device. You can reset the advertising ID in your device settings and request deletion of your server data at yeop2ad@gmail.com. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Effective date: November 3, 2026.
